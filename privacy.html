How SLExam Pro handles information and privacy

Privacy Policy

At SLExam Pro, we respect the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website.

📌 Information We Collect

SLExam Pro does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to browse most of the website.

However, certain information may be automatically collected by standard web technologies, including browser type, device information, IP address, approximate location, pages visited and general usage information.

🍪 Cookies

SLExam Pro may use cookies and similar technologies to improve website functionality, understand website usage and provide relevant advertising.

Third-party advertising providers, including Google, may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar technologies when serving advertisements on this website.

📢 Google AdSense

SLExam Pro may use Google AdSense to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's advertising cookies may enable Google and its partners to serve advertisements based on a user's visits to this website and/or other websites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

SLExam Pro may use third-party services such as Google AdSense, analytics services, external websites and other technology providers.
