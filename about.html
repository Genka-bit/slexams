About SLExam Pro

Your Free Sri Lankan Educational Resource

🎓 About SLExam Pro

SLExam Pro is a free educational resource website created to help students, teachers, parents and learners in Sri Lanka find useful educational materials in one convenient place.

Our aim is to make educational resources easier to discover and access by organizing examination papers, marking schemes, educational information and other learning resources according to grade, stream, subject and term.

SLExam Pro is designed as an independent educational resource platform and is intended to support students alongside their school lessons, teachers, textbooks and other educational resources.

🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make educational resources easier to find, understand and use.

Students often need additional practice materials when preparing for school examinations. SLExam Pro aims to provide a simple way for students to discover relevant examination papers and other educational resources without having to search through many different sources.

We also aim to maintain a simple, accessible and mobile-friendly website so that educational resources can be accessed conveniently from different devices.

📚 What We Provide

SLExam Pro may provide access to a variety of educational resources, including:

✔ Grade 1–13 Examination Papers
✔ Term Examination Papers
✔ O/L and A/L Educational Resources
✔ Marking Schemes
✔ Educational News and Updates
✔ Educational Schemes and Learning Resources
✔ Educational Applications and Tools

The availability of resources may vary depending on the grade, subject and term.

👨‍🎓 Who Can Use SLExam Pro?

SLExam Pro is intended for students, teachers, parents and other learners who are looking for educational resources related to the Sri Lankan school education system.

Students can use the available resources for revision, practice and examination preparation. Teachers and parents may also use the resources as supplementary materials when supporting students.

🗂️ How Our Resources Are Organized

To make resources easier to find, SLExam Pro organizes examination papers and related materials using categories such as grade, stream, subject and term.

For example, users can select a grade and then select the relevant subject and examination term to find available papers.

This structure is intended to reduce unnecessary searching and help users reach the resource they need more quickly.

📖 Educational Purpose

The resources available through SLExam Pro are intended for educational, revision and informational purposes.

Examination papers and other learning materials can be used as practice resources to help students understand question formats, review lessons and identify areas that may require additional study.

Students should use these resources together with guidance from their teachers, school lessons, textbooks and official educational materials.
