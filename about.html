🎓 About SLExam Pro

SLExam Pro is a free educational resource website created to help students, teachers and parents easily find useful Sri Lankan school examination papers and educational resources online.

Our platform provides educational resources for students from Grade 1 to Grade 13, including Ordinary Level and Advanced Level related materials.

📚 What We Provide

SLExam Pro provides access to a growing collection of educational resources, including:

✔ Grade 1–13 Examination Papers
✔ Term Examination Papers
✔ O/L & A/L Educational Resources
✔ Marking Schemes
✔ Education News and Updates
✔ Educational Applications

We organize resources by grade, subject, stream and term to make them easier to find and use.

🌟 Our Mission

Our mission is to make educational resources easier to discover and access for students and teachers across Sri Lanka.

We aim to continuously improve the website, organize educational materials clearly and provide a simple, mobile-friendly experience.

🤝 Our Commitment

SLExam Pro aims to provide useful and accurate educational information. We regularly improve the website and update available resources.

If you believe that any material on this website should be corrected, updated or removed, please contact us through our Contact page.
